735. Asteroid Collision

Turns out space problems can also be stack problems.

The key observation: a collision only happens when:

stack[-1] > 0 and asteroid < 0

In other words:

→ meets ←

Then compare their sizes:

* Same size → both explode
* Stack asteroid is larger → current asteroid explodes
* Current asteroid is larger → pop the stack and keep checking

That last case is the interesting one.

For:

[10, 2, -5]

2 collides with -5 → 2 explodes.
But -5 survives, so it immediately meets 10 → -5 explodes.

Final result: [10]

This is why the collision logic needs a while, not just an if.

Time: O(n) — each asteroid is pushed/popped at most once.
Space: O(n).

Reusable pattern:

When one incoming element may repeatedly eliminate previous elements, think stack + while loop.

Asteroids explode. The amortized complexity survives.
